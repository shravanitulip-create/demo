# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a positive and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to create an open and welcoming environment where everyone feels safe to participate, contribute, and learn.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or insulting comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Personal attacks or inappropriate language.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and other communication channels.

It also applies when an individual officially represents the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly and appropriately.

Project maintainers are responsible for taking suitable action based on the nature and seriousness of the violation.

The privacy and safety of individuals reporting incidents will be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining the consequences of any action that violates this Code of Conduct:

**1. Correction**

A private or public warning for inappropriate behavior. The individual may be asked to correct their behavior.

**2. Warning**

A formal warning with consequences for continued inappropriate behavior.

**3. Temporary Ban**

A temporary restriction from interacting with the community for repeated or serious violations.

**4. Permanent Ban**

Permanent removal from community participation for repeated violations or serious misconduct.
